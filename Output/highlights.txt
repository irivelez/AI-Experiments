Definitions:
- Telemanitoreo: Telemonitoring, a tool that can improve healthcare by remotely monitoring patients' health.
- Teleconsulta: Teleconsultation, the provision of healthcare services through remote communication technology.

1. Telehealth - The use of digital technologies to deliver healthcare services remotely.
2. Telemonitoring - The use of technology to monitor and track patient data remotely.
3. Artificial intelligence - Technology that enables machines to imitate human intelligence and perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.
4. Machine learning - A subset of artificial intelligence that allows machines to learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed.
5. Data privacy - The protection of individuals' personal information and sensitive data from unauthorized access or use.
6. Data security - Measures taken to protect data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction.
7. Digital health platforms - Technology platforms that enable the delivery of healthcare services and information.
